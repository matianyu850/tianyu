# Knowledge state - After chapter 11

## Known to all present
- The central tower sends usable/living vitality downward through a hidden eighth feed path toward the underground gate system.
- Historical tower-lighting trials made the pit quieter for longer while the gate/deep-pressure indicator rose across successive measurement rings.
- Crescent predecessors treated corpse concentration into the pit as cleanup because tower noise decreased afterward; this observation was mistaken for proof that the practice was safe.
- The tower currently has three relevant supply paths for feeding: blood-storage feed, guardian-contract/live-lock feed, and corpse/pit-chain feed.
- Cutting all three stopped the central tower feed and stopped dead beings from marching into the pit.
- Stopping the central feed weakens outward tower suppression: the east auxiliary tower went fully dark and other auxiliary towers are beginning to flicker.
- Large blue-eyed living corrupted creatures are approaching from the eastern forest; the old guardian says the towers previously suppressed them from entering.

## Known to specific characters
- Luo and Zhuzhao can see the guardian-contract shadow line and the tower-added black branch/black knot; the Crescent woman cannot.
- Luo learned he can divert the guardian-feed backflow into himself through blood/contact with the contract shadow, at physical cost.
- Zhuzhao learned the blood-vault dragon-paw wheel also acts as a feed shutoff when turned in the opposite direction.
- The old guardian distinguishes two functions: the pit suppresses/contains something inside, while the towers suppress threats outside.

## Unknown / unresolved
- What exactly consumes the vitality beneath the gate, and why feeding appears to stabilise the system temporarily.
- Whether stopping the feed will eventually weaken, strengthen, open, or change the underground gate.
- Whether the First Royal Heir is truly Zhuzhao's brother or a tower-generated residue using authentic memory.
- The true origin/purpose of the six bottles of royal blood.
- The number, nature, and capabilities of the external living corrupted creatures.
- Whether all six auxiliary towers will fail without central feeding.

## Clues found
- Nine historical measurement rings: after each tower-lighting trial, outward calm duration increased while the gate/deep-pressure indicator progressively rose; the ninth hit its limit.
- Crescent log note: 'concentration followed by reduced tower call' — an observation without proven causation, later institutionalised as corpse-disposal practice.
