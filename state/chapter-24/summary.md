# Chapter 24 Canon Delta — 《把灯灭了》

## Accepted chapter
- Chapter 24 is accepted Canon immediately after Chapter 23.
- POV remains third-person limited, primarily 洛沉川.

## Immediate events
1. 洛沉川、烛昭、闻契、桑枝 leave 七塔谷 immediately for 边陲村 while 第五门 is already sounding.
2. 第二影 still bears `五` from `空位递补`; it follows the old road rather than 洛沉川.
3. White light appears under the old cart ruts. Cutting the old-road line disrupts 第二影 briefly, but the fifth-door mechanism can re-identify a newly made route.
4. The group reaches 边陲村. Of the roughly 17 people associated with the settlement, 16 are physically present; 12-year-old 阿满 disappeared after answering a voice asking who knew the route to 荒田.
5. The village's fixed white light is being consumed from the south side toward the dragon-egg field.
6. New rule discovered: 第五 corresponds to `引` / guidance. It is using the white fixed light left by 烛昭 as a route-marker tied to 门主/王嗣 under `王归`.
7. To break that route-marker, 烛昭 voluntarily retracts the fixed white light she created in the village. 洛沉川 assists by prying out the original condensed dragon-blood anchor beneath the晒场石碾.
8. The condensed golden-white blood returns into 烛昭. The permanent fixed white-light sanctuary at 边陲村 is fully extinguished.
9. This is irreversible for current state: 边陲村 no longer has a self-sustaining fixed white safe zone. Residents must rely on finite dragon-blood lamps and likely evacuate toward 七塔谷.
10. 洛沉川 provides two bottles from his carried dragon-blood stock for temporary lamps / movement and explicitly treats them as debt/loan in character.
11. Breaking the fixed-light route only `断引`; it does NOT close 第五门.
12. 第二影's chest mark changes from `五` toward `引` after the fixed-light route is broken.
13. 阿满 returns to the old street with no visible shadow, carrying an unlit old lamp marked `五`.
14. 阿满 explains that a figure inside told him he knew the road and asked whether he wanted to go home. He answered `想`; the figure treated this as assent.
15. A real fifth-door manifestation begins opening behind 阿满; grey-white hands with black-silver fingertips appear, matching the class of beings previously seen under the underground wing-mark door.
16. 第五门 is therefore an `引门`: it recruits/uses an `引路者` to bring something from inside to outside.
17. Final chapter problem: although the fixed-light route is severed, 阿满's assent remains an active guide-link. The next task is to recover/cancel his `想` before fully closing 第五门.

## World / rule discoveries
1. Old numbered doors are not necessarily fixed to a single visible physical doorway; they can operate through route-lines, legacy anchors, and relational roles.
2. 第五 corresponds to `引` (guidance / bringing-through).
3. 第五门 can recognize both an existing route and a newly created route.
4. A permanent light left by 门主/王嗣 can function as an `引` route-marker under `王归`.
5. `王归` remains imposed by the door system based on烛昭's presence, regardless of her consent or self-identification.
6. Breaking a route-marker (`断引`) is distinct from closing the door (`闭门`).
7. Fifth-door recruitment can convert an apparently ordinary statement of desire (`想回家`) into assent without the target understanding the contract.
8. The recruited guide can return shadowless and carry a numbered `五` lamp.
