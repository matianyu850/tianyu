# Chapter 25 Canon Delta — 《路是要还的》

## Accepted chapter
- Chapter 25 is accepted Canon immediately after Chapter 24.
- POV remains third-person limited, primarily 洛沉川.

## Immediate events
1. Chapter opens immediately in 边陲村 old street with 第五门 already being pushed open behind 阿满.
2. 阿满's unlit `五` lamp activates without flame; a pale route/shadow trace appears while his true shadow remains collateral inside the lamp.
3. The recruiting voice (`前引`, descriptive role only; true identity still unknown) insists that 阿满's answer `想` constitutes assent to guide it homeward/outward.
4. 洛沉川 forces the exact wording into the open: 阿满 only said he wanted to go home; he never knowingly agreed to guide the recruiter, the grey-white beings, or surrender his shadow.
5. The `五` lamp is opened by 闻契. It contains 阿满's hair and a thin black shadow-fragment, establishing that the lamp holds his missing shadow as route collateral.
6. 第二影 supplies a limited rule: `偿引 / 路归路 / 引者带回所引`. It does not become omniscient.
7. 洛沉川 deliberately lets one grey-white black-silver-fingered being cross the threshold after exposing/cutting a white control line hidden in the door frame. This turns the abstract guide debt into a concrete `所引` relation.
8. 洛沉川 substitutes himself as current guide (`代引`) so 阿满 is no longer the disposable guide. The cost imposed by 第五 is `失路`.
9. As 洛沉川 walks the guidance route, his knowledge of routes/wayfinding is stripped: he still recognizes individual places and people, but loses the connective mental map that tells him how locations link together. He cannot independently navigate back to 七塔谷 at chapter end.
10. Evidence shows the grey-white being is not simply an invader allied with the recruiter: a black-silver chain under its tongue runs back into the door, and the recruiting voice demands it be returned. The being appears to be escaping a controlled/contained status.
11. Because the grey-white being only actually crossed after 洛沉川 cut its threshold control line, the guidance debt transfers away from 阿满 toward 洛沉川's action. 阿满's shadow returns fully and responds normally to his movements.
12. The grey-white being tears the tongue-chain/`引` tag from itself. 洛沉川 breaks the returned `引` tag at the threshold.
13. 第五门 closes. The recruiting voice remains unidentified and is not confirmed dead/destroyed.
14. 闻契 returns 洛沉川's black-silver short blade before the door fully closes; it remains in 洛沉川's inventory.
15. A human-looking hand with an old red cloth at the wrist throws out a black-silver piece just before closure. The piece bears `四` on one side and `拒` on the other.
16. 第二影's `引` mark breaks after 第五 closes, then `空位递补` activates again: its chest takes `四`.
17. A distant fourth-door sound begins from the north/darkness. 第四 is provisionally associated with `拒`, but the exact rule is not yet established.
18. The grey-white escapee remains alive at the village edge, badly injured with silver-white blood loss. It repeatedly says `萨维`, but 烛昭 concludes only that the term is likely not her personal name; exact meaning remains unknown.
19. The escapee draws an egg, a door containing many small figures, and a dragon outside, then blacks out the door with its own silver-white blood. This is a clue, not yet an explained rule.
20. The permanent village white light remains extinguished; the settlement still relies on finite dragon-blood lamps.

## World / rule discoveries
1. Fifth-door assent can be challenged by exposing the gap between what was actually said/known and what the door/recruiter claims was agreed; however argument alone does not close the door.
2. The `五` guide lamp can hold a guide's shadow as collateral via hair + shadow fragment.
3. `偿引`: a guidance debt can be settled/reassigned through the actual `引者` / `所引` relation.
4. `代引` is possible; it carries a cost to the substitute guide.
5. Fifth-door cost discovered in this use: `失路` — stripping connective route/wayfinding memory while leaving recognition of individual places intact.
6. The recruiting voice/`前引` is not proven to be identical with the grey-white beings. At least one grey-white being is controlled by a tongue-chain and attempts to escape rather than cooperate with the recruiter.
7. A hidden white control line in a door manifestation can bind a crossing entity to the threshold; cutting it can change who actually counts as having guided that entity across.
8. Closing 第五 can break 第二影's `引` assignment but does not end `空位递补`; the sequence advances to `四`.
9. New clue: `四` is associated with `拒`, but exact mechanics/price/assent condition remain unknown.
