## Irreversible developments
1. 第四门 mechanism is now partially established: `拒即界`.
   - Explicit verbal refusal can create a black boundary corresponding to the rejected action/relation.
   - Nonverbal refusal can also count when intent is clear.
   - Refusal can block touch, aid, light, or relational access rather than merely reverse a yes/no proposition.
2. Repayment rule observed: `受所拒`.
   - A refusal can be dissolved when the refuser positively accepts the previously rejected thing/action and the other side also receives/accepts that relation when required.
   - Positive phrasing is safer than negation; ordinary words such as `别/不要` can become dangerous when they clearly reject something.
3. 第二影's `四` develops into `拒`; it self-identifies as partial `拒门影` and can `记拒`, but cannot freely cancel refusals because `拒者自立`.
4. Refusals accumulate. Seven unresolved refusals create a stronger fourth-door manifestation; after seven have occurred/been registered, the door can continue to exploit the pattern even after some are repaid.
5. 洛沉川 discovers `代拒`: he can take another being's active refusal burden onto himself. Cost: his body/relations temporarily enact the borrowed rejection until he directly `受` the rejected contact/aid.
6. 洛沉川 assumes the grey-white escapee's three refusal burdens to keep it from being dragged out of the village and allow treatment. This explicitly reinforces his protective tendency through debt/account language.
7. 烛昭 accidentally says `那别救了`, creating a refusal boundary between herself and洛沉川 because he is the active person trying to save the grey-white escapee. She repairs it by positively stating `我要你救它`; 洛沉川 explicitly accepts the request as an `委托`.
8. During repayment of the borrowed refusal against烛昭,洛沉川's body involuntarily recoils from her light/touch despite his conscious intent. He endures direct contact until the refusal line breaks. Their protective contract line remains intact and identifies the current pair, but can be visually blocked/interfered with by refusal effects.
9. Grey-white escapee survives the chapter's treatment and accepts桑枝's bandaging after refusing earlier attempts. Its silver-white bleeding slows.
10. New clue for `萨维`: the grey-white escapee points first to烛昭, then later to洛沉川, and finally draws two people separated/not separated by a door with a hand/link symbol; when烛昭 asks `萨维?`, it explicitly nods. Exact translation remains unresolved, but it likely refers to a relation/link/function rather than烛昭's personal name.
11. Fourth-door manifestation can speak in familiar voices and offer desirable propositions specifically designed to provoke refusal.
12. 烛昭 avoids refusing the proposition `要不要我留下` by positively stating `我要你跟我走`.
13. The fourth door accepts that positive request and weaponizes the acceptance itself: it pulls烛昭's contract line north and proposes to make洛沉川 `follow` via the door.
14. A human-looking hand with old red cloth at the wrist appears again from the fourth-door manifestation. This matches the red-cloth wrist hand that threw the `四 / 拒` piece at第五 closure.
15. Fourth door asks洛沉川 `你拒绝吗?`; refusing would create a refusal debt, while not refusing allows the door's route/offer to operate.
16. 洛沉川, who still suffers `失路`, steps onto the door-created route and says `我接`, using the door's route rather than refusing it.
17. Consequence: 第二影's `四/拒` mark breaks and changes to `受`.
18. Final revelation/hook: the red-cloth hand names洛沉川 `第四位`; the door says `拒者已满，该收受者了`, and the true fourth door begins opening. This establishes that `拒` is only the first phase of the fourth-door mechanism; a new `受` phase is active.
