## Fourth-door discoveries
- `拒` is only the first phase. After seven registered refusals, the door enters `受` phase and designates a `第四位` / receiver.
- A black bowl with seven marks is used to re-present things/actions/relations that were previously refused, dropped, abandoned, pushed away, or left unclaimed.
- The door attempts to treat `我接` as unbounded acceptance, but洛沉川 demonstrates scope matters: acceptance requires some form of delivery/assignment, and prior context can be argued.
- `受者收所拒`: door tries to assign rejected/abandoned things to the designated receiver.
- What can be transferred includes at least `债、物、意、诺`.
- Items with a genuine prior owner can be reclaimed by that owner through positive reception;烛昭 reclaims rejected white light,桑枝 reclaims her mother's old copper clasp,阿满 positively takes back his route-memory.
- Door records the fact of a refusal even after a specific refusal boundary was previously repaid.
- `受` can also be satisfied when another party voluntarily accepts a relation/burden:烛昭 and the grey-white escapee perform a hand/paw linking gesture; this dissolves the re-presented tongue-chain burden.
- `萨维` is further indicated as a relational/linking concept rather than a personal name; exact translation remains unresolved.
