## Core events
1. 第二席 demonstrates `换` by automatically exchanging the active guide-role between 闻契 and 洛沉川. 洛沉川 temporarily receives 闻契's route knowledge while 闻契 temporarily experiences `失路`.
2. The trial exchange ends when the substituted task is completed; 洛沉川's original `失路` returns and 闻契 regains his own route knowledge.
3. 第二影 explains only after demonstration that `互代` can become eligible for exchange. 第二席 attempts to reinterpret the witnessed `互托` between 洛沉川 and 烛昭 as exchangeable substitution.
4. Name ownership/custody is NOT exchanged. The true wooden full-name anchor remains physically under 烛昭's neck scale; name still belongs to 洛沉川 and custody remains voluntary/bilateral.
5. Door-position exchange is offered. 洛沉川 is tempted to take 门主 burden from 烛昭, but 烛昭 refuses the exchange and explicitly objects to 洛沉川 treating every removable burden of hers as a cheap cost he should take.
6. To satisfy 第二席 without swapping name/blood/door identity, 洛沉川 and 烛昭 scope the exchange to `位置/身位` for passage through the seat.
7. `身位` exchange produces temporary cross-sensation: 洛沉川 feels 烛昭's four-footed stance, tail/horn bodily map, white-light pressure and name-anchor discomfort; 烛昭 experiences 洛沉川's old wounds and the phenomenology of `失路`. Physical bodies do NOT exchange.
8. During the exchange, their motor intentions begin to bleed across the intact contract line, but neither permanently substitutes for the other. Each still makes their own decisions.
9. The exchange resolves as only `一步`. 第二席 records the pair as `各自`; reverse-side `换` is struck through. The name arrangement survives unchanged.
10. 第二影's `二` collapses and reforms as `一`.
11. 第一席 is materially revealed as a vertical black-water surface. It reflects 洛沉川 and 烛昭 but not 闻契; 第二影 says 第一席 does not record旁证.
12. Inside the water is a second, highly convincing 洛沉川 with a complete name-response, one normal shadow, and seven shadow-forms behind him. The reflected 烛昭 has no wooden name-anchor under its neck scale.
13. When 烛昭 calls `欠钱的`, both the physical 洛沉川 and the water-side 洛沉川 answer `嗯` simultaneously.
14. The contract white line forks: one branch still connects 烛昭 to physical 洛沉川, while a second branch crosses the water to the other 洛沉川. This is the first event showing the contract recognizes both.
15. Chapter closes with the water-side 洛沉川 stating: `第一席只问一件事。谁是真的。`
