- `你娘等你很久了` was admitted bait; 烛昭's mother is NOT physically present in the house.
- A black scale-backed book was delivered long ago by a person missing/without effective use of the right arm. Exact identity is unknown.

- `离巢` is not presented as a road home.
- After departure, `归家屋` disappears, leaving only the fourth empty bowl.
- The bowl shows `一`, which begins transforming into a tiny door emitting gray/pre-dawn light.
