## Core progression
1. The warm house after First Seat is identified by its keeper as `归家屋`.
2. The old man initially claims `你娘等你很久了`, then admits he used the claim to induce 烛昭 to enter. 烛昭's mother is NOT physically present in the house.
3. The fourth bowl is explicitly associated with the extra shadow layer merged into 洛沉川's normal shadow after First Seat.
4. A thick black scale-backed book waits in the inner room. The keeper says it was delivered long ago by a one-armed/right-arm-missing person. This is NOT proven to be 守六 and must not be conflated with 守六.
5. Existing clues remain separate: Chapter 6 records an elf with `右臂失用`; Chapter 8 reports a right-arm-missing elf climbed from the pit twelve days earlier. Whether either is the book courier is UNKNOWN.
6. The black book normally rewrites under 王嗣 white light. To preserve its old page, 烛昭's light is suppressed under ordinary charcoal cloth; the wooden full-name anchor is NOT removed or surrendered.
7. 洛沉川 refuses to trade or expose the name-anchor merely to read the book, reaffirming that the name is more valuable than the information.
8. The group physically moves the whole low table and book outward rather than separating the book from its support. No ownership transfer of the book occurs.
9. Before the reading, they eat real meat from the house. 烛昭 visibly relaxes and resumes more talk; this meal does not create a stated magical debt.
10. The recorded female voice does not ask to be accepted as 烛昭's mother. It states that the egg carried no fixed name and warns 烛昭 not to let others define what she must be.
11. Major contract clue: if 烛昭 wakes with a white line, the recording says it is not simply an imposed lock; it is something she first grasped from inside the egg. Exact identity of the first grasped counterpart remains UNKNOWN.
12. Major name clue: the recording says if a gatekeeper entrusts a name to 烛昭, custody does not equal ownership, and she should not return it to the doors on their behalf while they cannot decide for themselves.
13. Major route clue: the recording warns that keeping a gatekeeper alive is insufficient if the doors remove every way they can leave. This directly resonates with 洛沉川's active `失路` state.
14. Major contract principle: `黎明契约不是谁替谁死`; at least one participant must still remember that the other can make their own movement/choice. Treat this as a recorded claim, not yet independently proven cosmic law.
15. The recording says reaching `归家屋` means the earlier seats have been passed and advises asking whether the people outside the home can still leave, rather than asking whether the home is true.
16. The recording cuts off after `如果不能——`; missing continuation remains an open clue.
17. A final page produces a white route-token/page marked `离巢`.
18. 洛沉川 pays one copper for the `离巢` page and a second copper for the meat rather than accepting them as free gifts.
19. `离巢` gives 洛沉川 exactly ONE navigable route despite ongoing `失路`. It does NOT restore his general route sense to 七塔谷, 边陲村, 北沟, or other locations.
20. The route is explicitly understood as left by the recorded woman and as NOT a road home. Its endpoint is still unnamed.
21. The group leaves `归家屋`; the old man remains behind, saying he waits for the next person who thinks the place feels like home.
22. After thirteen-plus steps, the house disappears completely. The old man, fire, smoke, and building are no longer visible.
23. A single empty fourth bowl remains behind.
24. The merged inner shadow in 洛沉川's normal shadow remains present; it has NOT been lost in the house.
25. The empty bowl displays `一`, then the mark starts changing into a tiny door.
26. Behind the tiny door appears gray light — the first explicitly observed light resembling pre-dawn gray rather than 烛昭 white, tower blue, or ordinary fire.
27. Chapter ending: 洛沉川 still knows the `离巢` route and realizes its far end is becoming bright.
