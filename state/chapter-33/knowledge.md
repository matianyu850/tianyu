# Knowledge state - After Chapter 33

## Known to 洛沉川、烛昭、闻契
- The fourth bowl's `一` becomes a tiny gray door that can pass small objects.
- A stone passed through once returns warm; a second stone attempt does not immediately return. Exact rule/price is unknown.
- The gray door initially shows wet soil, pale green grass and gray overhead light; this is observation, not proof that the place is a normal outside world.
- The merged inner shadow has limited independent agency and can write simple responses; it says return from the tiny door is `不知` and later resists being pulled through.
- Lifting the bowl removes the immediate ground-shadow pull, suggesting the mechanism depends partly on the door's relation to a surface/shadow, but this is not established as a universal rule.
- The bowl-door can temporarily fold/connect two demonstrated points on the `离巢` route. Rope and stone tests verify this specific connection.
- This does NOT prove global `失路` is cured or that the door can connect arbitrary places.
- The `离巢` paper-token is consumed by the iron threshold at the route endpoint.
- Beyond the threshold is an inhabited walled city under gray ambient light, with smoke, streets, residents and a central low black tower carrying a gray mirror.
- A warning sign reads `黎明封闭期间，严禁开门`. This proves only that the local warning uses the term `黎明`; it does NOT prove the city itself is named 黎明 or that the gray light is objectively dawn.
- After crossing the threshold, the bowl cracks but the gray door remains suspended independently.
- A second black hand appears from the far side of the gray door while the merged inner shadow remains present in 洛沉川's own shadow.
- The gray-mirror tower visually reacts; a knock from the gray door is answered by the tower; city residents then move toward the platform.

## Known specifically to 洛沉川
- The single `离巢` route was navigable throughout the descent despite global `失路`.
- The route-token no longer remains in inventory after being swallowed by the iron threshold.
- He consciously rejects treating the bowl-door as proof that routes to 七塔谷/边陲村/北沟 have returned.

- The meaning of the circle-plus-vertical mark written by the hand.
- The function of the gray-mirror tower.
- The meaning and scope of `黎明封闭`.
