## Chapter 33 progression
1. The bowl's `一` completes its transformation into a small gray door emitting pre-dawn-like light and damp-earth air.
2. The merged inner shadow independently reaches toward the door and can write minimal answers (`不`, then `不知`).
3. The gray door attempts to pull the merged inner shadow through. 烛昭 uses her light to stretch 洛沉川's shadow and help him move; the inner shadow ultimately resists and clings to his ordinary shadow rather than crossing.
4. 洛沉川 lifts the bowl. With no ground-surface beneath the bowl-door, the immediate shadow-pull stops. The party carries the bowl externally tied to his pack.
5. As they descend `离巢`, surrounding terrain develops wet earth/fine roots and ambient gray light. 烛昭 can suppress her own white light while still seeing the environment and sees a long externally cast shadow.
6. The bowl-door later displays the same stone steps / iron threshold lying ahead on `离巢`. Rope and stone tests demonstrate that it folds/connects this specific route segment.
7. The party DOES NOT treat this as restoration of general navigation. 洛沉川 explicitly retains the distinction between the single `离巢` route and global `失路`.
8. The `离巢` paper-token is inserted into and consumed by the iron threshold. The threshold opens.
9. The trio crosses onto a high stone platform overlooking an inhabited walled city beneath broad gray light. No visible sun is established; the sky/light's nature remains unknown.
10. Once 洛沉川 crosses, the fourth bowl cracks into four suspended shards, but the gray door survives independently in midair.
11. The merged inner shadow remains demonstrably in 洛沉川's ordinary shadow. Separately, a matching black hand reaches from the far side of the detached gray door, creating a new unresolved duplication/anomaly.
12. The far-side hand draws a circle-with-vertical mark and points toward a central low black tower topped by a gray mirror.
13. The mirror reacts as though opening an eye; smoke across the city shifts; residents stop and look toward the trio.
14. A corroded warning sign is read as `黎明封闭期间，严禁开门`. Do NOT reinterpret this as proof the city's name is 黎明.
15. The gray door knocks once; the gray-mirror tower answers once.
16. Residents begin converging toward the platform while the iron threshold behind the trio begins closing.
