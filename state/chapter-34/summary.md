# Chapter 34 Canon Summary — 《城里的人没有影子》

## Start
Immediate continuation from Chapter 33 on the high stone platform. Iron threshold is closing; residents converge; detached gray door and far-side black hand remain present; gray-mirror tower has answered.

## Confirmed events
- Residents initially halt and organize around the unauthorized gray door rather than simply attacking the trio.
- A gray-clothed middle-aged woman functions as a local responder/interpreter but is explicitly NOT omniscient.
- City residents under current gray light cast no ordinary shadows. Each carries a round gray mirror; their stated practice is that survivors of a prior opening put their shadows into mirrors and cannot retrieve them.
- Local warning/tradition claims `这里封的是黎明，不是城`; what `黎明` actually is remains UNKNOWN.
- Local woman claims opening can produce an extra shadow and that extra shadows seek people who still have shadows. She has direct evidence only through a second-opening case involving her missing brother; third-opening lore is not confirmed firsthand.
- The iron threshold can be held by a local gray mirror. When city mirrors are involuntarily reoriented toward the central tower, the threshold closes fully.
- 烛昭's white light is captured/remembered by city mirrors after the central gray mirror reacts. Covering 烛昭 afterward does not erase the recorded white image.
- The local woman identifies 烛昭 only through inherited/recorded white-dragon imagery, not personal recognition; she says old mirror records show a very large white dragon, but she has not personally seen the original record.
- The detached gray door begins closing. 洛沉川 uses the already cracked/disabled `五` lamp as a physical wedge, preventing complete closure at chapter end. This further damages the lamp; it remains wedged in the gray aperture.
- The merged inner shadow warns minimally `别 / 回 / 我`, then indicates the far-side hand is only `像`, not confirmed identical/self.
- Contact between the merged inner shadow and black seepage at the wedged gray door gives 洛沉川 a brief vision: the same city in darkness, streets full of shadows without people, and a right-arm-missing figure in/on the gray-mirror tower. This is a vision, not independently verified history.
- The local woman calls that figure `最后一个有影子的守镜人`, says local custom does not preserve gatekeeper/mirror-keeper names because names allow the mirror to find them, and claims he `把黎明关了` and left his own shadow behind the iron door. These are local claims, not universal law.
- Gray-mirror tower audibly/psychically calls 洛沉川 by his incomplete-access name: he cannot perceive/access the first character, but hears `沉川`; 烛昭 also recognizes the call.
- One young male resident's round mirror cracks; previously recorded 烛昭-white leaks onto the ground and produces a shadow that does NOT follow its owner. The shadow then begins crawling toward/grabbing the owner.
- Far-side black hand points at that new shadow and performs a throat-cut gesture.
- Local responders demand that 烛昭 use white light to kill the shadow.
- 洛沉川 physically places himself between 烛昭 and the crowd, refuses immediate compliance, and trades help for information about the right-arm-missing mirror-keeper.
- Chapter ends when the rogue shadow grabs the young man's ankle and 洛沉川 calls `烛昭`; her white light comes up under the cloak.
