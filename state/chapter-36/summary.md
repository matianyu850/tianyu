# Chapter 36 Canon Delta — 《右手不是你的》

## Accepted events
- Immediate continuation from accepted Chapter 35: seven tower locks have fallen; the physical right hand from inside the mirror tower is gripping 洛沉川's shadow and asks for its `right hand` back.
- Merged inner shadow recognizes only the `hand`, not the person; identity of mirror-keeper remains separate from 守六 / black-book courier / other right-arm clues.
- Tower occupant states that when sealing `黎明`, a mirror-keeper shadow was split: one portion remained inside to recognize the mirror system; one portion went outside to recognize people. This is current testimony, not yet universal law.
- Inner shadow autonomously chooses to LEND its right arm to the tower occupant; 洛沉川 explicitly refuses to decide for it. Loan is voluntary, not transfer of ownership.
- With borrowed shadow-right-arm, tower occupant stops the total gray mirror system. City mirrors begin releasing shadows; some immediately recognize owners, some do not.

- Borrowed shadow-right-arm is RETURNED in the same chapter. Inner shadow regains complete right arm and re-merges with 洛沉川's ordinary shadow.
- The tower door opens. Mirror-keeper's flesh right arm is physically intact; what was missing was the corresponding shadow-right-arm. Therefore local description `缺右臂` is only appearance/folk description for this specific figure and MUST NOT be generalized to other clues.
- Inside the tower, mirrors show locations from current journey (边陲村 / 七塔谷 / 第四供室 / 荒田) under an emerging gray line.
- Tower occupant claims current gray-city closure has lasted only `37 days` from his perspective, conflicting with local ~80-year tradition. Treat as unresolved temporal discrepancy.
- Final claim: the group did not merely arrive at `黎明`; `黎明` is leaking into places crossed by the door-shadow. Tower occupant points specifically to 洛沉川's merged inner shadow, not 烛昭, and warns that taking it back toward 七塔谷 may spread the leak.
