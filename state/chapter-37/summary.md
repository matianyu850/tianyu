# Chapter 37 Canon Delta — 《漏的不是影》

## Chapter objective achieved
- Tests the mirror-keeper's Chapter 36 claim instead of canonizing it unverified.
- Establishes that leaving 洛沉川's merged inner/door-shadow in the gray city does NOT stop gray-horizon leakage at previously traveled locations.
- Reconnects the unresolved cracked `五` lamp + detached gray door from Chapters 33–34 to the active leak.
- Reveals a separate far-side black humanoid entity is holding the damaged `五` lamp and the consumed `离巢` route-paper, and is using the group's traveled routes to open doors.

## Key causal sequence
1. Tower mirrors show gray horizons continuing to widen while the inner shadow remains physically merged with 洛沉川 in the tower.
2. Inner shadow states it does not remember ordinary roads; it recognizes doors.
3. 烛昭 recalls the fourth-bowl gray door opened ahead of the group before the shadow traversed the final route, weakening the simple `door-shadow causes leak` theory.
4. Group remembers the cracked `五` lamp was left wedged in the detached gray door on the high platform.
5. Mirror reconstruction shows the far-side black hand gripping the lamp and pulling it toward the far side; each opening movement correlates with widening gray leakage.
6. Inner shadow explicitly does NOT recognize the far-side hand/entity.
7. Group decides to return to the high platform rather than abandon the inner shadow in the city.
8. Mirror-keeper sells 洛沉川 a one-use gray mirror shard for one copper and removes a blooded First-Door mirror-key from his own chest, leaving him temporarily unable to leave the tower.
9. Inner shadow autonomously places a small `door mark` on the mirror-keeper so it may possibly locate him again; this is its own action, not 洛沉川's decision.
10. Outside, city shadow-return remains chaotic. 烛昭 tells a child with two candidate shadows not to choose immediately, echoing prior Seat lessons.
11. 洛沉川 uses the one-use gray shard. It shows the far-side entity now partly through the detached gray door, holding the consumed `离巢` paper transformed into a network of routes linking 边陲村 / 七塔谷 / 荒田 / 第四供室 / gray city.
12. The entity pulls the cracked `五` lamp completely free, opens the gray door wide, places the route-paper against it, and opens a door toward 七塔谷中央塔.
13. One-use shard breaks after this observation.

## Irreversible chapter-end state
- The detached gray door is now OPEN WIDE rather than held at a narrow gap.
- Damaged `五` lamp is no longer wedged in the gray door; it has been taken by the far-side entity.
- `离巢` paper, previously consumed by the iron gate, is now confirmed in the far-side entity's possession in altered route-map form.
- A door toward 七塔谷中央塔 has begun opening from the far side.
- Group is moving immediately back toward the high platform, with 闻契 still required as practical navigator because 洛沉川's global `失路` persists.
- Mirror-keeper remains in gray mirror tower to manage city shadow chaos; 沈砚秋 chooses to remain there.

## Interpretation limits
- Do NOT canonize `inner shadow leaves door marks that cause leaks` as universal law. Current evidence supports only that traveled doors/routes are exploitable from the far side.
- Do NOT merge the far-side black entity with 洛沉川's inner shadow, the mirror-keeper, 守六, the black-book courier, or any other right-arm clue.
- The far-side entity is currently unnamed and visually unresolved.
