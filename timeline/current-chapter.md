# 《黎明契约》Timeline

## Chapter 7 — northward movement / time unclear
- Luo Chenchuan and Zhuzhao leave the old relay-station light anchor and follow dead beings drawn north.
- They encounter the Crescent-marked group and enter an ancient underground channel.
- Zhuzhao activates a line of old green stones; an enormous wing-marked gate beneath the channel begins to open.

## Chapter 8 — immediately after Chapter 7
- The underground wing gate opens partially; gray-skinned beings beneath it attempt to close the gate while darker hands pull from below.
- Luo and Zhuzhao retreat with Crescent wardens through the old channel and emerge into a seven-tower valley surrounding a giant pit.
- Dead/corrupted beings are drawn into the pit.
- Crescent wardens reveal that a one-armed elf climbed out of the pit about twelve days earlier.
- The elf, visually matching the old guardian's missing right arm and black-silver equipment, watches Zhuzhao from the central tower.

## Chapter 9 — directly after Chapter 8, continuous rain
- Luo and Zhuzhao verify that the one-armed elf holds authentic private memories of the old egg-guardian.
- The recalled guardian demonstrates that he cannot freely cross the Crescent chain boundary; crossing accelerates corruption.
- He asks Zhuzhao to return to the old guardian system; she does not cross to him and remains with Luo.
- A tower-chain failure allows blue-eyed dead and a gray-skinned being to emerge from the pit edge.
- Luo's guardian contract and blood activate an old black-silver chain socket, restoring chain line three.
- The old guardian transfers a three-groove guardian key-ring to Luo; green and white grooves activate, third remains dark.
- Luo and Zhuzhao jointly activate the central tower living lock.
- The tower door opens a finger-width and reveals a third bodiless shadow between their own shadows.

## Chapter 10 — immediately after Chapter 9
- Luo and Zhuzhao enter the central tower with the third shadow and discover the confirmed tower blood vault: 323 ordinary dragon-blood bottles plus six small royal-blood bottles.
- The third shadow uses three stored shadow-residue placeholders to temporarily complete its missing limb, then returns that shadow into a black juvenile dragon skeleton in the tower heart.
- A black-silver plate identifies the skeleton as 'First Royal Heir'.
- The First Royal Heir residue speaks through the skeleton, calls Zhuzhao 'the second' and claims to be her older brother; the claim remains unverified.
- Historical Crescent notes show royal blood can light the tower but an old note says not to try again.
- The First Royal Heir warns them not to ignite royal blood and reveals that the tower is not closing the underground gate; it is feeding it.

## Chapter 11 — directly after Chapter 10
- Luo verifies a hidden downward feed path beneath the tower heart; repeated historical tower-lighting trials correlate with longer outward calm but progressively higher gate/deep-pressure readings.
- Crescent records show dead beings were concentrated into the pit for roughly twenty years because tower calls weakened afterward, without proof that the practice was safe.
- The central tower begins attempting to harvest/replace the old one-armed guardian after recognising Luo and Zhuzhao as a new guardian/live-lock pair.
- Three feed paths are identified and shut in coordination: blood-storage feed (Zhuzhao), corpse/pit-chain feed (Crescent warden woman), and the tower-added guardian-contract branch (Luo).
- Luo takes contract backflow into himself, reopens his palm wound, and severs only the tower-added black branch; the underlying guardian contract with Zhuzhao remains intact.
- Central tower feeding stops. Underground impacts stop for the moment. Dead beings cease marching into the pit and disperse.
- The old one-armed guardian is no longer actively harvested by the tower, but his existing corruption remains irreversible.
- The east auxiliary tower goes fully dark and another begins to flicker, revealing that the tower network also suppressed large living corrupted creatures outside the valley.
- Multiple large blue-eyed living corrupted beings begin approaching from the eastern forest. Luo chooses to build a physical defence instead of relighting the tower with royal blood.
