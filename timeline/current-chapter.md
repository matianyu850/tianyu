# 《黎明契约》Timeline

## Chapter 7 — northward movement / time unclear
- Luo Chenchuan and Zhuzhao leave the old relay-station light anchor and follow dead beings drawn north.
- They encounter the Crescent-marked group and enter an ancient underground channel.
- Zhuzhao activates a line of old green stones; an enormous wing-marked gate beneath the channel begins to open.

## Chapter 8 — immediately after Chapter 7
- The underground wing gate opens partially; gray-skinned beings beneath it attempt to close the gate while darker hands pull from below.
- Luo and Zhuzhao retreat with Crescent wardens through the old channel and emerge into a seven-tower valley surrounding a giant pit.
- Dead/corrupted beings are drawn into the pit.
- Crescent wardens reveal that a one-armed elf climbed out of the pit about twelve days earlier.
- The elf, visually matching the old guardian's missing right arm and black-silver equipment, watches Zhuzhao from the central tower.

## Chapter 9 — directly after Chapter 8, continuous rain
- Luo and Zhuzhao verify that the one-armed elf holds authentic private memories of the old egg-guardian.
- The recalled guardian demonstrates that he cannot freely cross the Crescent chain boundary; crossing accelerates corruption.
- He asks Zhuzhao to return to the old guardian system; she does not cross to him and remains with Luo.
- A tower-chain failure allows blue-eyed dead and a gray-skinned being to emerge from the pit edge.
- Luo's guardian contract and blood activate an old black-silver chain socket, restoring chain line three.
- The old guardian transfers a three-groove guardian key-ring to Luo; green and white grooves activate, third remains dark.
- Luo and Zhuzhao jointly activate the central tower living lock.
- The tower door opens a finger-width and reveals a third bodiless shadow between their own shadows.
